# Global and community reputation

The platform will show a Community Solidarity Score for each membership and a Global Solidarity Score for the person. The global score will combine community scores with weights proportional to the person's completed item and help exchanges in each community: 400 completed exchanges in one community and 20 in another give those communities a 20:1 influence. A completed exchange counts once for each participant; posts, thank-yous, and vouches do not add weight. Communities with no completed exchanges have no global weight, and a person with no completed exchanges displays 50 — Unproven globally. This preserves local context while allowing a person's demonstrated history to inform trust elsewhere. Unresolved disputes and confirmed serious violations appear separately from the numeric score so a low-volume community's incident is still visible to an owner. Across communities, the indicator reveals type and status but withholds private evidence, messages, and community identity from nonmembers.
