# Mutual Aid Community Platform

Language for the private community sharing and help system.

## Community and membership

**Community**:
A private group whose members share resources, offer help, and govern participation within that group.
_Avoid_: Collective as an internal term

**Community Member**:
A person approved to participate in a particular community.
_Avoid_: User when referring to community-specific rights

**Community Administrator**:
A community member entrusted with membership decisions, moderation, and dispute review for that community.
_Avoid_: Platform administrator

**Invite**:
An administrator-issued path for a person to request membership in a community; membership still requires administrator approval.
_Avoid_: Vouch

**Vouch**:
A member's statement that they personally trust another person to participate responsibly in their community.
_Avoid_: Invite, membership approval

## Sharing and help

**Listing**:
An offer by a member to share an item or provide help to their community.
_Avoid_: Need Post, Borrow Request

**Need Post**:
A member's open request to their community for an item or help, without applying to a specific item listing.
_Avoid_: Request

**Borrow Request**:
A member's application to borrow a specific item listing for a proposed period.
_Avoid_: Need Post, Request

**Exchange**:
A coordinated instance of one member sharing an item or providing help to another member.
_Avoid_: Transaction

**Qualifying Interaction**:
A completed exchange counted once for each participant when weighting their communities in the Global Solidarity Score.
_Avoid_: Post, thank-you, vouch

**Unproven**:
The displayed standing of a community member with no completed exchanges in that community, or of a person with no completed exchanges across all their communities.

**Handoff**:
The point when a shared item passes from its owner to its borrower, with its condition acknowledged.

**Return**:
The point when a borrowed item passes back to its owner and its condition is confirmed or contested.

## Trust and governance

**Community Solidarity Score**:
A member's reputation within one community, derived from their recorded participation and conduct there.
_Avoid_: Treating it as a person's score in every community

**Global Solidarity Score**:
A person's reputation across their communities. Each community's influence is proportional to the person's qualifying interaction count there.
_Avoid_: Currency, credit balance

**Reputation Event**:
A recorded reason for a change in a member's Community Solidarity Score.

**Dispute**:
A reported disagreement about an exchange that requires a recorded resolution.

**Community Disciplinary Vote**:
A community decision process concerning whether to suspend or remove a member.
_Avoid_: The Gulag as an internal term
